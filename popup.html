📋 14 Strict Rules Reference
  1. Website Status: Working / Not Working (Do not work on redirect/subdomain; purchase / shopping cart sites mark as Not Working; Not Working means all other fields empty).
  2. Business Type: Manufacturer to Wholesaler. (Shopping with manufacturing = Manufacturer).
  3. Company Profile: Available / Not Available.
  4. Company Name: Full form, remove brackets, Title Case, strip special chars.
  5. Country Name: Full form, Title Case (England/Scotland/Wales for UK).
  6. Company Address: Full form, remove postal code, country, company name, special chars.
  7. State: Available / Not Available (Explicit State/Province/County or Capital only).
  8. City (Critical QC Rule): Available ONLY if explicit recognized Town, District, or Municipality. STRICTLY Not Available for Suburb, Industrial Area, Business Park, Locality, or Street only.
  9. Email ID: First corporate email.
  10. Secondary Email ID: Mandatory field (fill if present, skip if not).
  11. Phone Number: Remove country code, spaces, special chars. No toll-free, no leading zero.
  12. Number of Products: "One" if 1, "Three" if >3.
  13. Product Images: "One" if 1, "Three" if >3.
  14. Product Descriptions: "One" if 1, "Three" if >3.
