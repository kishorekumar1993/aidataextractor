We Didn’t Step Away—We Stepped Forward


You may have heard EarthCruiser closed its factory doors. But this isn’t the end of our journey—it’s the beginning of a bold new chapter.

With all direct customer orders now fulfilled, we’re shifting gears. We’re no longer just building vehicles—we’re building a movement. One grounded in craftsmanship, innovation, and a deep commitment to empowering the next generation of builders, explorers, and overland travelers.

Why the Shift?


For nearly two decades, EarthCruiser has set the benchmark in expedition-ready overland vehicles. From the legendary EXP to the versatile MOD and rugged Terranova platforms, we’ve built hundreds of trucks that have traveled millions of miles—across continents and climates, from jungle treks to desert crossings to epic ski trips.

What once sat on the fringe of the RV industry is now a thriving market, powered by people who want quality products without a ridiculous price tag. Instead of scaling the traditional way, we’re doing what EarthCruiser Innovation does best: breaking the mold.

It’s time to remake the RV industry—by supporting local manufacturing across North America. We’re teaming up with van upfitters and independent builders—the very folks who’ve led innovation and customer experience in this space, just like we have.

Introducing Our Platform Program

We’re kicking things off by offering our two most in-demand vehicle platforms as fully engineered, ready-to-install shells:

  • MOD Slide-In Camper
  • Terranova Fixed-Chassis Unit
  • Limited builds of EXP and FX available by request

 

These platforms are designed for independent van upfitters, custom builders, and conversion specialists to finish the interiors and accessories based on your customer’s style, needs, and budget.

Need a little help and some free advice ? We’re happy to share the insights we’ve gained over years in the field—check out our tutorials and walkthroughs on our YouTube channel.

And this is just the beginning.

The long- awaited EarthCruiser EVADO for the Sprinter chassis is on the way, along with towable options crafted specifically for overland travelers, sportsmen, and adventure seekers alike.

Because It Matters

We’re not here to mass-produce RVs. We’re here to redefine what a modern manufacturing company can be—rethinking what the builder–dealer ecosystem looks like.

Our builder partners love what they do. It’s their passion. They bring authenticity, dedication, and craftsmanship to every build— put to work for you. When you buy from one of our partners, you’re supporting local manufacturing and getting a better product, built to last and serve you the way it should.

Innovation continues at our Bend, Oregon headquarters, where we’re actively developing next-gen platforms and pushing the boundaries of off-grid exploration.

And coming soon: Tag-Along Training Tours— guided adventures where EarthCruiser owners can gain hands-on skills, build confidence, and join a thriving community of like-minded explorers.

EarthCruiser EXP

Featuring a cab-over design, a powerful 6.6L V8 gas engine, and a four-wheel-drive system made specifically for overland travel, the EarthCruiser EXP delivers smooth highway driving and exceptional capability when the road ends and the adventure begins.

Its innovative lifting roof—originally designed for international containerized shipping—adds both practicality and spaciousness for life on the road. Enjoy a low-profile, aerodynamic design while driving, and an open, airy living space when parked, thanks to the automatic roof lift.

The EarthCruiser EXP is available in both single and dual cab configurations, offering the ultimate in expedition-ready overland travel.

There are four vehicle slots made available each Calander year mixed between EXP and FX.

EarthCruiser FX

Sharing its legendary DNA with the renowned EarthCruiser EXP, the EarthCruiser FX features a fixed roof design while retaining the same trusted systems, driveline, and floor plan. Introduced in 2015 in response to customer demand, the FX was created for those eager to explore the vast and diverse landscapes of North America.

The EarthCruiser FX offers the same rugged performance and thoughtful engineering as its cab-over sibling, making it a reliable and comfortable choice for long-term overland travel—with the simplicity and strength of a fixed roof

There are four vehicle slots made available each Calander year mixed between EXP and FX.

Terranova

A true four-season overlander built on a domestic one-ton chassis with an expanding roof. Excellent highway performance and towing capacity without sacrificing off-road capability. Available on the Ford F-350 or Ram 3500 in gas or diesel.

EarthCruiser MOD

The EarthCruiser MOD is a lightweight, four-season overland camper engineered to fit any pickup truck sold in North America. No matter your truck bed size, there’s a MOD that fits.

One of its standout features is the innovative forward storage compartment, available for truck beds longer than 5.5 feet. This optional storage solution comes in two sizes and adds both form and function to your expedition setup.

Built for those who want to explore unbound—without sacrificing comfort, durability, or drivability—the MOD is an overland platform that’s as capable off-grid as it is around tow
